A little about me

Public health with people at the centre.

My work and learning sit at the intersection of community health, research, and accessible digital solutions.

I am a Public Health Technology graduate of the Federal University of Technology, Owerri, with practical exposure to immunization campaigns, digital data collection, health education, and healthcare settings. I am interested in how trustworthy information and thoughtfully designed technology can help individuals and communities make informed health decisions.

I continue to build my skills through research training, professional development, global health networks, and participation in digital health initiatives. I value collaboration, accurate data, clear communication, and solutions shaped by the needs of the people they serve.
